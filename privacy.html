Global Privacy & Data Rights Policy

Effective Date: October 2026 • Compliant with GDPR (EU 2016/679) & CCPA/CPRA

1. Commitment to Reader Privacy

ApexPulse Global ("ApexPulse", "we", "our") is dedicated to upholding the highest standards of data integrity and digital privacy. As an independent research publication, we adhere to the principle of strict data minimization.

2. Information Collection

We do not collect personally identifiable information (PII) unless you explicitly transmit an inquiry via our contact form or subscribe to our newsletter. Anonymous telemetry (such as device type, browser user agent, and aggregated geographic region) is processed solely for network load balancing and programmatic advertisement verification.

3. Advertising & Third-Party Partners

Our digital properties integrate with verified programmatic advertising syndicates (including Monetag) to support independent journalism. These networks may utilize cookies or web beacons in compliance with standard IAB TCF 2.2 guidelines.

4. Your Statutory Data Rights

Under GDPR and CCPA, you retain the legal right to request access, rectification, or total deletion of any telemetry or communication logs associated with your IP address. Contact our compliance desk at privacy@apexpulse.top.
